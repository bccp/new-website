## The BCCP Partner Fund

![Sather Gate, the green and white ornamental entrance to the UC Berkeley campus, with students walking through it on a sunny day.](/images/sather-gate.jpg)

Unlocking the mysteries of the universe is an exciting scientific challenge, and it depends on visionary funders who are willing to invest in the future of science. As a partner of the BCCP, you can help us illuminate the infrastructure of the universe and nurture the next generation of promising scientists.

The very human desire to know, to understand, to be part of a grand intellectual journey is motivating a remarkable quest. The concentration of talent at the BCCP is dedicated to teaching as well as research. This creates a unique opportunity to involve today's educators in engaging and inspiring the next generation of physicists, engineers, mathematicians, teachers, and citizen-scientists.

An investment in the BCCP is not only an investment in the future of cosmology. It also means creating a model of inquiry that can usher in a new era of 21st century science - with the utility and power to solve very complex problems. Having pioneered data analysis procedures for extremely large data sets, cosmological methodologies have great potential for many other data intensive fields. Cosmology research created an enormous impact on many disciplines - seeding advances in mathematics, chemistry, engineering, physics, astronomy, particle physics, and biology.

BCCP's frontier research and education depends on private philanthropy from UCB alumni and friends, corporations, and foundations. Gifts to Berkeley play an essential role both in sustaining fundamental traditions of excellence and in moving the University forward decisively.

To make a donation, please contact the BCCP Director, Uroš Seljak, at [useljak@berkeley.edu](mailto:useljak@berkeley.edu) or the BCCP Administrative Assistant, Maria Feng, at [mariafeng@berkeley.edu](mailto:mariafeng@berkeley.edu).
